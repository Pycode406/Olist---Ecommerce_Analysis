### Business context

Olist is a Brazilian marketplace integrator connecting small and medium-sized sellers with multiple online marketplaces. The dataset contains approximately 99,441 orders covering September 2016 to October 2018, across nine relational tables involving orders, customers, sellers, products, payments, reviews and geolocation.
The central business problem is that these dimensions exist separately, making it difficult to understand what is actually driving customer satisfaction and operational performance.

The analysis therefore focuses on six questions:

How are orders, revenue and reviews changing over time?
How does delivery performance affect satisfaction?
What patterns exist across sellers and geography?
Which product categories perform better or worse?
How does payment behavior relate to order value and customer experience?
What are the primary drivers of low review scores?
